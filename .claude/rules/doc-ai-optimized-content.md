# Rule: AI-optimized content

Structured, semantically meaningful content lets AI systems interpret and retrieve answers
accurately, and improves discoverability in search engines and internal tools.

- Clear, descriptive headings — see `doc-formatting-and-typography.md`.
- Break long paragraphs into ordered or unordered lists.
- Short, declarative, concise sentences — see `doc-plain-language.md`.
- Consistent formatting: tables, admonitions, text callouts, and code blocks.
- Direct and action-oriented, organized around what the reader wants to do. Avoid ambiguous phrasing and vague, product-centric language — see `doc-voice-and-tone.md`.

## Procedures as steps, not prose

Never bury a procedure inside a sentence.

- Not this: "Creating an alert involves opening the alerts pane, selecting **Create alert**, and then configuring the alert criteria."
- This:

    To create an alert, follow these steps:

    1. Open the **Alerts** pane.
    2. Select **Create alert**.
    3. Configure the alert criteria.
